01 / The story

In a city where
faith meets power.

After personal loss in medieval France, the blacksmith Balian discovers that a visiting Crusader is his father, Baron Godfrey of Ibelin.

He travels to the Holy Land, inherits Ibelin, and becomes part of a fragile peace between King Baldwin IV's Jerusalem and Saladin's Ayyubid forces. When Guy de Lusignan and Raynald of Châtillon provoke war, Balian must defend civilians and the city itself through the siege of 1187.

07 / Historical context

The film
and the history.

Kingdom of Heaven builds a fictional journey inside the political and religious tensions that led to the Third Crusade.

HistoricalBaldwin IV, Saladin, Hattin, the 1187 siege, and the negotiated surrender of Jerusalem.
FictionalizedThe historical Balian was a prominent lord, not a French blacksmith; Godfrey is an invented father figure.
Reading the filmIts message of coexistence and conflict reflects both medieval events and modern debates about religion and power.
